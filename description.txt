About the job
REMOTE IT INTERNSHIP


Note: This internship is independent from any federal contracts or client projects. Interns will work on simulated, portfolio-ready projects created by DS Systems. This program is best suited for college students looking to gain experience, build real-world tech projects, and operate in a remote, corporate-style environment designed for early-career growth.
Status: Unpaid
Location: Remote (U.S.-based applicants only)
Duration: 5 Weeks
Eligibility: Must be enrolled in a 2-year associate’s or 4-year undergraduate program


About the Program
The DS Systems Internship Program is a hands-on, portfolio-driven opportunity designed for college students pursuing careers in technology, cybersecurity, government consulting, or federal IT.
Over five weeks, interns will complete five guided projects—one each week—ranging from coding and data visualization to real-world IT strategy simulations. Each project is added to a public portfolio page hosted on our website, giving interns a platform to showcase their work to future employers.
This is more than just a resume-builder. It’s a launchpad.


What You’ll Gain
✅ 5 real projects to showcase publicly in a professional portfolio
✅ Mentorship from senior professionals in government contracting
✅ LinkedIn referrals & recommendation letters upon successful completion
✅ Brand-building support for your future in tech or federal consulting
✅ Featured profile on the DS Systems website
✅ College credit compatibility (check with your school advisor for transfer eligibility)
✅ Networking opportunities in the federal technology space


Qualifications
Must be actively enrolled in a U.S.-based 2-year associate or 4-year undergraduate program (any major welcome, tech interest preferred)
Strong written communication and time management
Passion for technology, problem solving, and innovation
No prior federal experience required — just curiosity and commitment
